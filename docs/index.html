News Lens

Cross-outlet coverage matrices for individual news stories. Each report shows how multiple outlets covered the same event side-by-side: who asserted what as fact, who quoted whom, which framings echoed and which diverged, and what was structurally absent from every article in the sample.

The reports are tools for reading, not verdicts. They make the shape of coverage legible; the judgment is yours.
